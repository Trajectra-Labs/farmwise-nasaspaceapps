# FarmWise: 30-second demo script

Goal: show real NASA data, three rotation options, scenario exploration and plain-language guidance in one pass. Total 30 seconds. Rehearse once; every click below is deterministic and offline-safe.

**Before recording:** open the app in a clean browser profile (or clear site data), light mode, window about 1440 x 900, zoom 100%. The page should show the welcome screen.

| Time | Screen | Do | Say (voice-over) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Welcome | Click **Try Sample Farm** | "FarmWise helps a smallholder farmer in Nakuru, Kenya decide what to plant next." |
| 4-10 s | Dashboard | Point at the NASA strip and the rainfall and soil cards | "It starts from real NASA POWER history, 2014 to 2023, clearly labelled as a snapshot. The soil sensor is labelled simulated." |
| 10-17 s | Rotation Planner | Open **Rotation Planner**, click **Compare all**, close, then choose the **Resilience-oriented** card | "Three complete rotations: current practice, a minimum change, and a more resilient one, each with a score, confidence and the reasons." |
| 17-23 s | Scenario Studio | Click **Use in scenarios**, then the **Drier** preset, nudge **Input cost change** | "Now a drier year or higher costs. Water, yield, cost and net return update, as ranged estimates, never guarantees." |
| 23-27 s | Assistant | Click **Ask FarmWise**, pick "What happens in my current scenario?" | "The assistant answers from this farm's own plan and scenario." |
| 27-30 s | Dashboard | Click **Dashboard** | "Back on the dashboard everything agrees. Data from NASA, decisions for the farmer." |

**Safe fallbacks while recording**
- If a click misses, press the browser back button; state is saved and nothing is lost.
- Voice input is optional and not used in this script.
- Plant Check (labelled demo images, no diagnosis) can replace the assistant beat if the video allows a longer cut.
- Do not say "real-time", "live", "forecast" or "guaranteed": the data are historical and the results are model estimates.
